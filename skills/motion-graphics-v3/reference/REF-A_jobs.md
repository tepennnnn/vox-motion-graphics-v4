## What defines the look
- **Three worlds, one system**: dark (skeleton, label cards), off-white paper with grid + blurred grey 3D shapes + leaf shadows, white satin. Each world holds 2-5 s, not 0.7 s like Editor V1. Pace comes from elements arriving INSIDE a held layout (word by word, cutout by cutout), not from hard cuts.
- **Hard cuts are rare** (diff spikes at f2-4 flash, f47, f135, f211, f365, f521). Everything else is a transition device: zoom blur, bar→card mask, blur dissolve, circle iris, bloom to white, desat+fade.
- **Cutout people** (background removed), B&W, high-contrast, layered 2-3 deep (same person at different ages), breaking out of a black card's top edge; cutouts enter by sliding in from the side or dropping from the top, exit by dropping out.
- **Real images in small doses**: real logo (NeXT cube) rebuilt 3D-ish, real product clip (iPhone), real posters (Pixar), one film-like stock clip.
- **Type system**: (1) bold grotesk heading (NEXT, PIXAR) (2) thin small sans for connecting words, landing one per spoken word (3) italic serif / script for the emotional keyword (precision., storytelling, Failure, Sharpened, rejection, restart) (4) huge stacked condensed white for the thesis line (HE RETURNED DIFFERENT) (5) tiny tracked caps between cards (A P P L E).
- **Design garnish everywhere**: barcodes, QR, "00004", "+" crosshairs, hatch stripes, arrows, dashed target rings, grids, a bottom "ticket"/spec label — gives the editorial streetwear-poster vibe.
- **Colour**: mostly monochrome (B&W photos, cream, black); ONE accent colour per video (here orange via the iPhone, gold trophy).
