## What makes it "fire" (vs the first three references)
- **Full-colour worlds that change every 2–3 s**: grey photo, white, red, black, studio white, charcoal, sky, teal, black,
  pink/white, black, orange, real photo, beige, black, white, black, green. Never the same world twice in a row.
- **Real objects PERFORM over the type**: a cloche lifts to reveal food, cheese spins in front of the words, money lands
  on the stat, a chart line threads through a glass case, words sit INSIDE a cart behind its wire mesh. The object and
  the words share one space.
- **Heavy extruded 3D type** (condensed black caps with a 6–12 px extrusion + soft shadow) for every headline.
- **One big gesture per line**: a snap zoom into a screen, ribbons swooping, a notebook rotating straight, a store rising.
- **UI acts**: a dialog types the line on a real laptop; a real website scrolls in a phone.
