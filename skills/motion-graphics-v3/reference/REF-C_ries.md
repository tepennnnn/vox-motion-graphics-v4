## What defines the look
- **Long continuous camera**: nothing is ever still; pans, drifts, rotation, whip pans. Scenes run 2-4 s, the page scene 4 s.
- **Real material is treated as texture**: article pages tilted, B&W, vignetted, highlighted; brand objects as clean cutouts on navy.
- **Light leaks are the transitions** between worlds (red/orange/yellow burns, a purple streak, flat colour slabs).
- **Two worlds**: warm grey paper (story/history) and near-black navy (ideas/explanation). Thin white strokes and thin light type on navy.
- **Captions = the VO verbatim**, each word blur→sharp on its onset, small (≈ 3-4% of frame height) except the hero words.
