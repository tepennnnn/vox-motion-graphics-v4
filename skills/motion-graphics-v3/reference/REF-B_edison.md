## What defines the look
- **One person, many real photos**: every shot is a real archive photo of Edison, all B&W except one warm colour painting near the end. Different ages = the story timeline.
- **Grey-on-grey monochrome**: background, text, cards, tape, all greys. Text colour is mid-grey with low contrast, so the photo is the hero.
- **Card + cutout**: a gradient card with a long soft shadow; the person rises/slides in inside it and breaks out of its top edge.
- **Depth sandwich**: ground → disc/card → words → cutout head. Words are often partly hidden by the head.
- **Hand-made marks**: pen arrows, circles, diagonal strokes drawing on; tape strips; handwritten letter ground.
- **Captions ARE the layout**: stacked, mixed weight (bold key words, thin small connectors), per-letter bounce, typewriter with cursor.
- **Transitions**: zoom-through blur, blur-cut, sliver-to-card, wipe-up photo stack. No hard cut without blur.
- **Pace**: scenes of 1.5-2.5 s, then accelerating to ~0.4 s per photo at the payoff.
